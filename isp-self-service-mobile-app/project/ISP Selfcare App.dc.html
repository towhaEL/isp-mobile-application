ISP self-service app

Redrawn on the Industry system: steel accent on a technical ground, square hairline objects with registration marks, condensed headings. Tap through the phone — every screen is live.

Assumptions
  • Residential fibre, postpaid monthly, BDT
  • One connection per login; ID is the account key
  • Bill, packages, usage and support carry the load — five tabs, no hamburger-only paths
  • Payment handoff to mobile wallets and cards
  • Bilingual copy (EN/BN) assumed at build time; screens shown in EN
